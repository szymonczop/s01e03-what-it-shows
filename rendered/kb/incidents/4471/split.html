  • parser: the shared parser reads every date month first, so a day-first tenant’s 7 April comes out as 4 July. This hits every tenant on file and every channel.
  • hint: the booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, then reads the date day first. People type what the hint says. The symptom is the same, but the cause and the fix are different. Only form bookings at booking-v3 tenants are affected.
  • one-day: the date moves by exactly one day, and day and month are not swapped. “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.” This looks like a time zone bug. The desk had already noted these reports in desk-notes.json.

Why they ended up in one ticket

  • Decision 0001 merges on subject line alone. All three problems arrive with the same subjects (“Wrong date again”, “Month and day swapped?”), so the body was never read.
  • Intake’s tag is guessed from the reporter’s browser, not looked up from the tenant. It is wrong in both directions, as the “intake tagged as” column shows. Count by tenant and form, as causes.ts does, and not by tag.
  • The ticket export repeats some reports across its pages. Count each id once.
